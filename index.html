About Me

I hold a Ph.D. in Mathematics, with a specialization in Riemann surfaces and complex geometry. My academic background includes differential and complex geometry, moduli spaces and mathematical structures.

In recent years, I have expanded my interests toward data science and statistical computing, particularly using R and RStudio. I am interested in connecting mathematical theory with computational methods and applications in finance.
